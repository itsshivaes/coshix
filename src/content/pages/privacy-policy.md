#### Introduction

Welcome to Coshix. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how Coshix collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website, including interactions with our blog posts, comment sections, third-party analytics, and advertising partners. By using our website, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

#### Responsibility of Contributors

Visitors, users, and contributors to Coshix must ensure that their interactions, submissions, and shared content remain respectful, lawful, and aligned with our community standards. Any comments, posts, or media that threaten, harass, or harm the reputation of any person or organization are strictly prohibited and subject to immediate removal. Contributors are solely responsible for the authenticity and legality of the content they publish or submit through our platform.

#### Gathering of Personal Information

We gather personal information when it is voluntarily provided by users, such as when subscribing to our newsletter, contacting support, or registering an account. Additionally, we automatically collect certain technical information when you navigate our site, including your browser type, operating system, IP address, and pages visited. 

To help us analyze site performance and user interaction, we use **Google Analytics**, which utilizes cookies to track traffic patterns, session durations, and referral sources. Furthermore, our platform integrates the **Disqus commenting system**, which collects profile data, email addresses, and identifiers when you choose to log in and participate in article discussions.

#### Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

Coshix uses third-party advertising vendors, including **Google AdSense**, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google and its partners use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website and other websites across the internet. 

* Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to Coshix and/or other sites on the Internet.
* Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting **[Google Ads Settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/)**. 
* Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting **[www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info)**.

#### Protection of Personal Information

All data collected through user interaction, comments, analytics, or advertising mechanisms is handled with strict administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards. We use browser local storage and encrypted cookies to maintain your site appearance preferences and cookie consent status, while ensuring unauthorized access to personal credentials or contact information is actively prevented.

#### Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, services, or tools that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

#### Children's Privacy

Coshix does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take steps to remove such information from our servers.

#### Privacy Policy Changes

1. All platform items, guidelines, and privacy terms are designed to stay up to date with the latest industry standards, legal mandates, and ad network requirements.
2. Comments or submissions that threaten or harm the reputation of any person or organization will be moderated, restricted, or permanently removed from public view.
3. Personal information, including but not limited to email addresses, telephone numbers, and profile data, is managed strictly under confidential and secure privacy guidelines.
4. Any update or modification coming to our technology, advertising policies, or data handling framework will result in an automatic notification or update posted directly on this page.
